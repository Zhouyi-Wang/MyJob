## 📐 Methodologies

### Multi-Criteria Decision Making
- **AHP (Analytic Hierarchy Process)** — Subjective weighting based on expert judgment
- **Entropy Weight Method** — Objective weighting based on data dispersion
- **TOPSIS** — Technique for Order Preference by Similarity to Ideal Solution

### Predictive Modeling
- **Logistic Growth** — S-curve forecasting for adoption/demand
- **Time Series Analysis** — Trend and seasonal decomposition

### Optimization
- **Linear/Nonlinear Programming** — Constrained resource allocation
- **Queueing Theory (M/M/c)** — Service facility sizing and wait time analysis
- **Sensitivity Analysis** — Robustness testing via parameter perturbation
